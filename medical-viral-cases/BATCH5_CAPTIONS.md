onto a knife.

## 2. A nail had been in his brain for 12 YEARS 🔩

**Hook B:** *"Doctors found a nail in his brain. He'd forgotten to mention it."*

```
Doctors scanned his head for a stroke. They found a NAIL. 🔩🧠

He came to the ER with one side of his face drooping and weakness on one side of his body.
It looked like a stroke.

But the scan showed a long metal nail sitting deep inside his brain.

.
.
.

👉 It had been there for 12 YEARS.

Hours later he told doctors: during a severe depression 12 years earlier, he had been injured with a nail gun.
And then… he simply lived with it.
No seizures. No symptoms. For over a decade. 😳

Doctors say low-speed nail injuries like this can sometimes leave people with surprisingly few symptoms — but they should always be evaluated by neurosurgeons.

💙 If you're struggling, you are not alone. Please reach out — call or text your local crisis line (988 in the US) or talk to someone you trust.

## 3. 350 coins inside one man's stomach 🪙
📷 `03_350_coins_xray.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/medizzy-com-350-coins-ingestion/)
**Hook B:** *"5.5 kg of coins. Collected over 10 years. Inside his stomach."*

```
That white mass on the X-ray? It's MONEY. 🪙

A 62-year-old man in France came in with belly pain and swelling.
He couldn't eat. He couldn't go to the toilet.

The X-ray showed a massive bright shape at the bottom of his abdomen…

.
.
.

👉 350 COINS. Plus jewelry and needles.
Total weight: 5.5 kg (12 pounds). 😳

He had been swallowing them for about 10 years.
The weight had dragged his stomach down between his hips.

He had pica — a condition that causes cravings to eat non-food items (coins, chalk, hair, soil…), often linked with mental illness or nutritional deficiencies.

Surgeons removed everything… but sadly, he died 12 days later from complications. 💔

🩺 Pica is real and treatable. If someone you know eats non-food items, it's a medical issue — not a quirk.

Had you ever heard of pica? 💬

## 5. The MRI showed the baby's legs OUTSIDE the womb 🤰

```
Doctors had never seen anything like this MRI. 🤰😳

A 33-year-old woman, 22 weeks pregnant, felt completely fine.
But a routine ultrasound showed something impossible.

Look at the arrows 👇

.
.
.

👉 The uterus had a 2.5 cm TEAR.
The amniotic sac had bulged out through it like a balloon (the big white area)…

The likely reason: she'd had 5 previous C-sections, which can weaken the uterine wall.

At 6 months, he was healthy and thriving. 💙

🩺 After multiple C-sections, regular prenatal scans are extra important.

## 6. The bullet in his knee slowly POISONED him 🔫
📷 `06_bullet_dissolved_lead.jpg` · Original: NEJM (Marquez et al., 2018) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/retained-bullet-led-to-lead-toxicity/)
**Hook B:** *"Left: the bullet after the shooting. Right: 14 years later."*

```
LEFT: a bullet in his knee after a shooting.
RIGHT: the same knee 14 YEARS later. 😳

Doctors had left the bullet in place.
14 years later, the 46-year-old came back with a swollen, painful knee.

The new X-ray: the bullet had BROKEN APART and spread through the whole joint like glitter. ✨

.
.
.

👉 And it was dissolving into his body.

Joint fluid slowly breaks down lead.
His blood lead level was 182 μg/dL — the kind of level that causes serious poisoning.
He also had anemia and memory problems on testing. 🧠

✅ He was started on chelation therapy (drugs that pull lead out of the body).

🩺 Bullets inside JOINTS are different from bullets in muscle — they often need to come out. Anyone living with a retained bullet should ask about lead testing.

Did you know a bullet could poison you years later? 💬

#Radiology #XRay #LeadPoisoning #Trauma

## 7. A baby's brain tumor had TEETH 🦷🧠
📷 `07_brain_tumor_with_teeth_mri.jpg` · Original: NEJM (Johns Hopkins, 2014) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/a-brain-tumor-containing-teeth/)
**Hook B:** *"Look at the top arrow. Now look at the bottom arrow. Same thing."*

```
Compare the two arrows. The bottom one points to normal baby teeth in the jaw.
The top one is INSIDE HIS BRAIN. 🦷🧠

A 4-month-old was brought in for a stuffy nose.
His doctor noticed his head had grown too fast in just 2 weeks.

The MRI showed a large tumor in the middle of his brain…
with little tooth-shaped structures around its edge.

.
.
.

👉 Surgeons at Johns Hopkins thought they were flakes of calcium.
Under the microscope, they were fully formed TEETH. 😳

The tumor was a craniopharyngioma — a type long suspected to arise from cells similar to the ones that form teeth.
Teeth inside one had only been reported a handful of times before.

✅ Surgeons removed most of the tumor. By age 2, he was walking and developing well. 💙

🩺 A baby's head growing unusually fast always deserves a check-up — measuring head size is part of routine baby visits for a reason.

Have you ever heard of a tumor with teeth? 🤯

#Neurosurgery #MRI #MedicalMystery

## 8. Chopsticks hidden inside her face for a WEEK 🥢
📷 `08_chopsticks_in_sinuses_ct.jpg` · Original: *Journal of Emergency Medicine* · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/woman-had-chopsticks-lodged-in-her-sinuses-for-an-entire-week/)
**Hook B:** *"A fight at dinner. 2 tiny cuts. A week later she saw something in her nose."*

```
A family fight at dinner. A few tiny cuts. Then she noticed the chopsticks were… shorter. 🥢

A 29-year-old woman in Taiwan was attacked with chopsticks by her sister at the dinner table.
She had a mild nosebleed and 2 small cuts under her eye and nose.
The X-ray in the ER looked NORMAL.

A week later, she looked in the mirror… and saw something grey inside her nose. 👃

.
.
.

👉 The CT (yellow lines): TWO pieces of chopstick buried deep in her face —
one through the nasal septum, one pushed up into the sinuses between the eye and nose.
Lengths: 3.5 cm and 5 cm. 😳

Plastic and wood often DON'T show up on X-rays.

✅ Both were removed surgically. She recovered completely.

🩺 Tiny puncture wounds can hide big objects. If something "went in," make sure ALL of it came out.

## 9. A bladder stone the size of an OSTRICH EGG 🥚
📷 `09_ostrich_egg_bladder_stone.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/urinary-bladder-stone-ostrich-egg/)
**Hook B:** *"770 grams. Removed from one man's bladder."*

```
This came out of one man's bladder. 770 grams. 🥚😳

A 64-year-old came to the ER with flank pain and trouble peeing.

10 years earlier, he'd had bladder cancer — surgeons removed his bladder and built him a NEW one from a piece of his intestine (a "neobladder").

The CT showed a giant white oval filling it 👇

.
.
.

👉 A STONE measuring 12 × 9.5 × 7.5 cm — about the size of an ostrich egg.

Why? Bladders made from intestine produce mucus and change the urine chemistry, and urine can sit there longer — perfect conditions for minerals to build up layer by layer.

✅ Surgeons removed it through an incision, and a smaller kidney stone with a laser. No complications.

🩺 People with a reconstructed bladder need lifelong follow-up — stones can grow silently.

Would you have believed a stone could get this big? 👇

#Urology #Radiology #CTscan #KidneyStones

## 10. The sailor who swallowed 35 knives (1799–1809) 🔪⚓

**Hook B:** *"These were all found inside one man. He swallowed them to win bets."*

```
Every object in this 200-year-old drawing came out of ONE man's body. 🔪⚓

1799. John Cummings, a 23-year-old American sailor, watched a street performer pretend to swallow knives.
That night, drunk, he bragged he could do it for real.

So he swallowed his pocketknife.
Then 3 more. 😳
He passed most of them… and felt fine.

.
.
.

Over the next 6 years he did it AGAIN… and AGAIN… to impress crowds of sailors.
👉 14 knives one night.
👉 17 knives on another voyage.

That last time, he described "a feeling of death."
For 3 years he vomited and passed knife fragments, in and out of hospitals.

He died in 1809. 💀 This historical illustration shows knife fragments from his case.

🩺 Real sword-swallowers use years of training and never actually swallow blades. Don't try to "prove it."

Craziest bet you've ever seen someone take? 💬
