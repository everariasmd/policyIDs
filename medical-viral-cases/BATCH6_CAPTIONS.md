**Hook B:** *"Stomach ache on the flight. Cardiac arrest at baggage claim."*

```
She complained of a stomach ache on the flight. At baggage claim, her heart stopped. ✈️💔

A 34-year-old woman arriving in France from Brazil suddenly started seizing while waiting for her bags.
Airport medics resuscitated her and rushed her to the ICU.

Her urine was positive for cocaine.
So doctors ordered an abdominal X-ray… 👀

.
.
.

👉 Her intestines were full of oval PACKETS.

She was a "body packer" — someone who swallows drug packages to smuggle them.
Surgeons removed 50 packets of cocaine.
5 had burst inside her — releasing a massive overdose straight into her gut. 😳

And one more twist: she was PREGNANT.

✅ She survived, went home 4 days after surgery, and months later gave birth to a healthy baby. 🍼

🩺 For "body packers," a single burst packet can be fatal within minutes.
```

## 3. He held in a sneeze, and it tore his throat 🤧
📷 `03_held_sneeze_ruptured_throat.jpg` · Original: BMJ Case Reports (Leicester Royal Infirmary) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/man-ruptures-throat-by-holding-a-sneeze/)
**Hook B:** *"Never pinch your nose and close your mouth to stop a sneeze. Here's why."*

```
STOP holding in your sneezes. This X-ray is why. 🤧

A 34-year-old man in the UK felt a sneeze coming…
so he pinched his nose AND clamped his mouth shut.

He felt a "POP" in his neck.
Then pain, a swollen throat, and he could barely swallow or speak.

See the dark streaks in the neck on the X-ray (arrow)? 👀

.
.
.

👉 That's AIR — leaking into his neck tissues.
The pressure of the trapped sneeze had TORN the back of his throat. 💥

A sneeze blasts air out at high pressure. With every exit blocked, it has to go somewhere.

✅ He was fed through a tube for several days so the tear could heal, and went home after a week.

🩺 Doctors warn it can also burst eardrums or, rarely, brain aneurysms. Just sneeze — into a tissue or your elbow. 💪

Be honest… do you hold in your sneezes? 😬

#Radiology #ENT #DidYouKnow
```

## 4. Half his chest was a giant air bubble 🎈
📷 `04_vanishing_lung.jpg` · Original: case report (Cocaine-Induced Giant Bullous Emphysema) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/giant-bullous-emphysema/)
**Hook B:** *"Doctors put in 2 chest tubes. Neither worked. Here's why."*

```
Look at the right side of this chest (red arrows). Where's the lung? 🫁

A 50-year-old man was found unresponsive.
In the ER, his right chest barely moved and doctors heard NO breath sounds on that side.

The X-ray and CT showed the right side almost completely BLACK — filled with air.
It looked like a collapsed lung.

So doctors inserted a chest tube to drain the air… nothing.
A bigger tube… still nothing. 🤔

.
.
.

👉 It wasn't air AROUND the lung.
It was a GIANT AIR BLISTER (bulla) growing out of the lung itself — so big it filled the entire right side of his chest.

Doctors call this "vanishing lung."
His likely cause: years of injecting and snorting cocaine — he had never smoked tobacco. 💨

✅ Surgeons removed the giant bulla. 8 days later, he went home breathing without oxygen.

🩺 Drugs like cocaine can silently destroy the tiny air sacs of the lungs.

Had you ever heard of "vanishing lung syndrome"? 💬

#Radiology #Pulmonology #CTscan
```

## 5. His liver was full of GAS 💨
📷 `05_gas_filled_liver_ct.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-emphysematous-hepatitis/)
**Hook B:** *"The dark bubbly area is where his liver should be."*

```
That dark, bubbly area on the left of the scan? That's his LIVER. Filled with gas. 💨😳

An 82-year-old man came in with 1 day of fever and confusion.
His heart was racing at 150, his blood pressure dropping.

The CT scan showed something doctors rarely see:
a huge part of his liver had been replaced by GAS.

.
.
.

👉 Emphysematous hepatitis.

Gas-producing bacteria (Klebsiella — a drug-resistant strain) had invaded the liver and were destroying it from the inside, creating gas as they spread.

It's extremely rare — and extremely deadly.

Despite powerful antibiotics, a drain and ICU care, he died 8 days later. 💔

🩺 Fever + confusion in an elderly person = get help fast. Infections can move frighteningly quickly.

Had you ever heard of gas forming inside an organ? 💬
```

## 6. The water he drank turned his bones to stone 💧🦴
📷 `06_fluoride_bones.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/skeletal-fluorosis-in-32-year-old/)
**Hook B:** *"Brown teeth. A stiff neck. Bones too dense on X-ray. One cause: his tap water."*

```
Brown-stained teeth. Bones like concrete. Both caused by his drinking water. 💧🦴

A 32-year-old man had years of back pain and couldn't move his neck, hips or shoulders properly.

Look at the photos 👇
🦷 (A) Yellow-brown staining on his teeth
🦴 (B) Pelvis X-ray: bones abnormally white and dense
🦴 (C) Forearm: calcium deposits forming between the two bones (arrowhead)

.
.
.

👉 Skeletal FLUOROSIS.

He lived in an area where the groundwater naturally contains very high levels of fluoride.
Years of drinking it made his bones abnormally thick but stiff, and calcified the ligaments around them.

✅ He switched to defluoridated water and got calcium and vitamin D — 2 years later, his pain and stiffness had improved by about 50%.

🩺 To be clear: this is from extremely HIGH natural fluoride levels in some regions' groundwater — not from normal toothpaste or treated tap water.

Do you know where your drinking water comes from? 💬
```

## 7. She died from drinking soy sauce 🧂
📷 `07_soy_sauce_brain_ct.jpg` · Original: published case report · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/soy-sauce-ingestion-led-to-death/)
**Hook B:** *"Too much SALT did this to a brain."*

```
These brain scans show what too much SALT can do. 🧂🧠

A 40-year-old woman with schizophrenia was healthy until a few hours before she arrived at the ER —
then came seizures, a fever over 40.5°C, and she wouldn't wake up.

Her blood sodium was dangerously high: 183 (normal is about 135–145).
Doctors couldn't find why… until her family explained:

.
.
.

👉 She had drunk 500 mL of TRIPLE-STRENGTH soy sauce. 😳

That much salt pulls water OUT of brain cells, making the brain shrink (left scan).
Then the brain swelled dangerously (right scan, day 3).

Doctors lowered her sodium carefully — but on day 4 she was declared brain dead, and she died on day 8. 💔

🩺 Salt poisoning is rare but real — especially in children and people with mental illness. Keep high-salt products and seasonings stored safely.

Had you ever heard of salt poisoning? 💬

#Toxicology #Neurology #CTscan
```

## 8. 6 tiny magnets: 2 in his throat, 4 in his belly 🧲
📷 `08_magnet_beads_xray.jpg` · Original: *Journal of Emergency Medicine* · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/3-year-old-swallowed-magnet-beads-that-got-stuck-in-his-throat-and-abdomen/)
**Hook B:** *"Parents: count the dots on this X-ray."*

```
Parents: look at this X-ray of a 3-year-old. See the tiny dots? 🧲

He swallowed 6 tiny magnet beads (3 mm each) from a toy while his sister was babysitting.

The X-ray: 2 stuck in his THROAT… 4 in his BELLY.

.
.
.

👉 The 2 in his throat had snapped together THROUGH a fold of tissue — right at the edge of his airway.
If one had come loose, it could have fallen into his lungs. 😳

Surgeons removed them in the operating room (they kept sticking to the metal forceps!).
✅ He went home the next day, and the 4 in his belly passed naturally 3 days later.

⚠️ Why magnets are SO dangerous:
2 or more swallowed magnets can pull together through the walls of the intestine — squeezing the tissue until it dies and tears.

🩺 If a child swallows MORE THAN ONE magnet — or a magnet plus anything metal — go to the ER immediately.

Do your kids have magnetic toys at home? 🔍
```

## 9. He fell into a grain wagon and inhaled WHEAT 🌾
📷 `09_grain_inhaled.jpg` · Original: case report (PubMed 9357907) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/massive-grain-aspiration-management/)
**Hook B:** *"Grain behaves like quicksand. This boy was buried for 7 minutes."*

```
These wheat seeds were pulled out of a teenager's LUNGS. 🌾🫁

A 16-year-old fell into a 3-meter-deep wagon full of wheat.
Grain behaves like quicksand — he sank instantly.
It took 5–7 minutes to pull him out.

He was blue, unresponsive, barely breathing.
Paramedics tried to ventilate him… but air wouldn't go in.

.
.
.

👉 His airways were PACKED with grain.

Even with a breathing tube, doctors couldn't push air past the wheat blocking his windpipe.
Using a rigid scope, they pulled out 5–10 seeds at a time, while squeezing and tapping his chest to shake more loose.

In total: about 200 mL of wheat — nearly a cup — removed from his airways.

✅ 9 months later: no breathing problems, no brain damage, normal lung tests. 🙌

🩺 Grain bins and wagons kill farm workers every year. Never enter one alone, and never while grain is moving.

Do you know anyone who works on a farm? Share this with them 🚜
```

## 10. "Coat hanger" ribs: a rare genetic sign on a newborn's X-ray
📷 `10_coat_hanger_ribs.jpg` · Original: *Journal of Perinatology* · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/coat-hanger-deformity-of-the-ribs/)
⚠️ *Infant death. Post with a gentle tone and moderate the comments.*
**Hook B:** *"Look at the shape of these ribs. Doctors call it the 'coat hanger' sign."*

```
Look at the shape of these tiny ribs. Doctors call it the "coat hanger" sign. 🦴

This X-ray belongs to a baby boy born at 28 weeks.
His rib cage is narrow, with the ribs crowded and bent like a wire coat hanger.

Before birth, scans had already shown extra amniotic fluid and an omphalocele (organs outside the belly in a sac).

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: Paternal uniparental disomy 14 — an extremely rare genetic condition.

Normally we get one copy of each chromosome from mom and one from dad.
In this baby, BOTH copies of chromosome 14 came from his father. 🧬

That imbalance affects how the chest, belly wall and other organs develop.
The "coat hanger ribs" are one of its signature signs on X-ray.

💔 Sadly, after 4 weeks on a ventilator, he passed away from complications.

💙 Sending love to every NICU family.

#Genetics #Radiology #NICU #RareDisease

📷 Journal of Perinatology / via MEDizzy
```
