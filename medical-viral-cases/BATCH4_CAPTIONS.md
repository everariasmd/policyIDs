## 2. The tumor that kept making him collapse ⚠️
📷 `02_giant_tumor_low_sugar.jpg` · Original: American Journal of Case Reports · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/recurrent-hypoglycemia-caused-by-a-solitary-fibrous-tumour/)
**Hook B:** *"He wasn't diabetic. So why did his blood sugar keep crashing?"*

```
He's 94, still played golf… then he fell multiple times in 24 hours. 🏌️

Paramedics checked his blood sugar: dangerously LOW.
They gave him glucose. It dropped again. And again. 📉

The strange part? He wasn't diabetic.
He wasn't on any sugar-lowering medicine.

.
.
.

👉 The cause was THIS — a giant tumor in his belly. 😳

Doctors had known about it for a year and were just "watching" it.
But it had grown — and some tumors like this one (a solitary fibrous tumor) can produce a hormone that acts like insulin, draining sugar from the blood.

His sugar kept crashing, especially at night.
So, at 94, he chose surgery.

✅ Within 24 hours of removing the tumor, his blood sugar was normal — and the episodes never came back. 💪

Would you choose major surgery at 94? 🤔

#Surgery #Endocrinology #MedicalCases
```

## 3. Blurry vision was the first clue to his leukemia 👁️
📷 `03_leukemia_retina.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/retinal-manifestation-of-leukaemia-retinal-haemorrhage/)
**Hook B:** *"An eye doctor looked at the back of his eye and called for blood tests."*

```
This is the back of a 14-year-old's eye. All that red is BLOOD. 🩸👁️

He'd felt tired and feverish for 2 weeks.
Then, for 3 days, his vision went blurry.

The eye exam revealed large pools of bleeding over his retina.

.
.
.

👉 Blood tests showed low red cells, low white cells, low platelets…
Diagnosis: Acute lymphoblastic LEUKEMIA. 💔

Leukemia crowds out healthy blood cells. With too few platelets, blood can't clot properly — and fragile vessels in the eye can bleed.

✅ With treatment, his vision improved significantly by 4 months.

🩺 Fatigue + fever + unusual bleeding or bruising + vision changes = get a blood test. The eyes can reveal disease anywhere in the body.

Did you know an eye exam could detect leukemia? 💬

#Leukemia #Ophthalmology #ChildhoodCancerAwareness

📷 via MEDizzy
```

## 4. The X-ray pointed to the WRONG lung 🫁
📷 `04_choking_xray_baby.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-foreign-body-aspiration-in-a-12-month-old/)
**Hook B:** *"The right lung looked collapsed. The problem was in the left."*

```
Parents, look at this X-ray. The baby choked while eating 4 hours earlier. 👶

A 12-month-old started coughing and making a high-pitched noise while breathing after choking at mealtime.
Now? Looked mostly okay.

The X-ray: the RIGHT lung looks white and collapsed.
So the problem must be on the right… right? 🤔

.
.
.

❌ WRONG.

Doctors found the object blocking the LEFT airway.
It acted like a one-way valve: air got IN to the left lung but couldn't get OUT.
The left lung over-inflated… and squashed the right one. 🎈

✅ It was removed through a scope.

🩺 Food and small toys often DON'T show up on X-rays. If a child chokes and then coughs, wheezes or breathes noisily — get them checked, even if they "seem fine."

🍇 Cut grapes, nuts and hot dogs for little ones.

Parents: what food scares you most for choking? 💬
```

## 5. He got a fungus from trimming trees 🌳 ⚠️
📷 `05_blastomycosis_skin.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-study-primary-cutaneous-blastomycosis/)
**Hook B:** *"Landscaper. Itchy bumps. 4 months. It was growing from the soil."*

```
He's a landscaper. For 4 months these bumps kept growing on his back. 🌳

Red, raised, crusted, itchy nodules — spreading across his lower back.
No cough, no fever, nothing else.

The clue was his job: trimming trees, with frequent scrapes and cuts.

.
.
.

👉 A biopsy showed budding FUNGUS cells (photo B).
Diagnosis: Blastomycosis 🍄

Blastomyces lives in moist soil and rotting wood.
It usually infects the lungs when people breathe in spores.
But in this case it entered directly through his skin — which is rare.

🩺 Outdoor workers: clean and cover cuts, wear gloves and long sleeves. A skin lesion that won't heal for weeks deserves a biopsy.

Do you work outdoors? Ever had a "mystery rash" from the garden? 💬

#Dermatology #FungalInfection #OccupationalHealth
```

## 6. She couldn't open her eye. The cause surprised doctors 👁️
📷 `06_eye_gonorrhea.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-occular-gonorrhea-in-24-year-old/)
**Hook B:** *"Red eye, yellow discharge, swollen shut. Not pink eye."*

```
"It's just pink eye." It wasn't. 👁️

A 24-year-old woman had 2 days of a red, painful eye with thick yellow discharge and blurry vision.
By the time she reached the ER, her eyelid was so swollen she couldn't open it.

The CT showed the infection had spread around the eye.
Under the microscope: the bacteria inside her white blood cells (photo B).

.
.
.

👉 GONORRHEA — in her EYE.

Yes, the sexually transmitted infection. 😮
Gonorrhea can infect the eye through contact with infected fluids (usually hand-to-eye).
And in her case, urine testing for genital gonorrhea was NEGATIVE.

⚠️ Eye gonorrhea is an emergency — untreated, it can damage the cornea and cause blindness.

🩺 Red eye with HEAVY yellow-green discharge and rapid swelling ≠ ordinary pink eye. Get seen fast.

Did you know gonorrhea could infect the eye? 💬

#Ophthalmology #SexualHealth #InfectiousDisease
```

## 7. The skin-graft wound that wouldn't stop growing ⚠️
📷 `07_giant_pyogenic_granuloma.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/giant-pyogenic-granuloma-on-the-left-thigh/)
**Hook B:** *"Doctors took skin from her thigh to fix her leg. Then her thigh did THIS."*

```
Doctors took skin from her thigh to repair an injury. Then THIS grew in its place. 😳

A 28-year-old woman badly injured her leg in an accident.
Doctors repaired it with a skin graft taken from her own thigh.

The leg healed beautifully. ✅
But the spot where they TOOK the skin from never healed. ❌

Instead, a painful, bleeding, foul-smelling mass grew there — so painful she was bedridden for 2 months.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: a GIANT pyogenic granuloma.

Despite the scary name, it's NOT cancer and NOT pus.
It's an overgrowth of tiny blood vessels — the body's healing process going into overdrive. 🩸

✅ It was cut out and covered with a new skin graft.

Have you ever had a wound that just wouldn't heal? 💬

#WoundCare #PlasticSurgery #Dermatology
```

## 8. A white pearl growing on a boy's eardrum 🦪
📷 `08_eardrum_cholesteatoma.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/cholesteatoma-tympanic-membrane-rupture/)
**Hook B:** *"A year after his eardrum tore, a white 'pearl' appeared on it."*

```
What's that white "pearl" on this eardrum? 🦪👂

A 12-year-old's eardrum had torn in an injury a year earlier.
It was patched and healed.

Then a small, round, white mass appeared right on the eardrum.
No pain. No hearing loss.

.
.
.

👉 A CHOLESTEATOMA — a cyst made of trapped skin cells.

When the eardrum tears, skin can get trapped as it heals and keep growing in the wrong place.
It's not cancer, but cholesteatomas can slowly ERODE the tiny hearing bones and nearby structures if ignored.

This one was caught early — tiny (3 mm), just touching the first hearing bone.

✅ Removed under a microscope. 6 months later: no recurrence. 🎉

🩺 After an eardrum injury, follow-up checks matter — even if it "healed."

Had you ever heard of a cholesteatoma? 💬

#ENT #EarHealth #MedicalCases
```

## 9. "Headlight in the fog": a parasite inside his eye 🔦
📷 `09_headlight_in_fog_toxo.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-acute-ocular-toxoplasmosis/)
**Hook B:** *"Doctors call this sign 'a headlight in the fog.'"*

```
Doctors have a name for this: "a headlight in the fog." 🔦🌫️

A 49-year-old man had blurry vision and glare in one eye for a week.
When doctors looked at the back of his eye, they saw it:
a bright white spot (arrow) glowing through hazy, inflamed fluid.

.
.
.

👉 Ocular TOXOPLASMOSIS.

Toxoplasma is a tiny parasite carried by cats 🐈 and found in undercooked meat, soil and unwashed produce.
Most infected people never have symptoms.
But it can attack the retina — and it's the most common infectious cause of inflammation at the back of the eye.

He was treated with antibiotics and steroids. The inflammation cleared… but his vision in that eye stayed at 20/200. 😔

🩺 Cook meat thoroughly, wash fruit & veg, wear gloves when gardening, and wash hands after the litter box.

Did you know a parasite from cats could affect the eye? 💬

#Ophthalmology #Toxoplasmosis #Parasites
```

## 10. His back pain and his blind eye had the same cause 👁️
📷 `10_eye_infection_from_blood.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-endogenous-endophthalmitis-in-44-year-old/)
**Hook B:** *"Fever. Back pain. Then his eye turned cloudy."*

```
Fever. Back pain for 2 weeks. Then his left eye turned red and cloudy. 👁️

The 44-year-old had diabetes and kidney failure.
In his left eye, the cornea was cloudy and pus was collecting at the bottom of the eye.
He could barely see light.

How do an eye and a back connect? 🤔

.
.
.

👉 Bacteria (Staph aureus) were in his BLOODSTREAM.
They had seeded his heart valve, the muscles of his back… and the inside of his eye.

Diagnosis: Endogenous endophthalmitis — an eye infection that comes from INSIDE the body, through the blood.

Despite antibiotics injected into the eye and 8 weeks of IV antibiotics, his vision did not improve. 💔

🩺 People with diabetes or on dialysis: sudden eye pain or vision loss with fever = emergency. Hours matter.

Did you know a blood infection could reach the eye? 💬

#Ophthalmology #Sepsis #Diabetes
```
