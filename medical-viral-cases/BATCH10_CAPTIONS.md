## 1. He arrived unconscious with THIS tattooed on his chest
📷 `01_do_not_resuscitate_tattoo.jpg` · Original: Holt et al., NEJM 2017 (via MEDizzy) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/do-not-resuscitate/)
⚠️ *The patient died. This is an ethics debate post, so moderate the comments respectfully.*
**Hook B:** *"Would YOU follow this tattoo if you were the doctor?"*
```
He arrived unconscious. No ID. No family.
Just these 3 words tattooed across his chest. 😳

A 70-year-old man was brought to a Florida emergency room.
He had serious lung and heart disease, and alcohol in his blood.
His condition was getting worse fast.

Then the team saw his chest: "DO NOT RESUSCITATE" — with what looked like his signature.

.
.
.

👉 The doctors faced an impossible question:
Is a tattoo a legal medical decision?
What if he got it on a dare… or changed his mind?

They called in the hospital's ethics team. Their advice: the tattoo most likely showed what he truly wanted — so honor it.

The team later found he HAD also signed an official Do-Not-Resuscitate form.
His wishes were respected, and he passed away that night without CPR. 🕊️

🤯 This case was published in one of the world's top medical journals because doctors genuinely didn't know what was right.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: in most places, a tattoo is NOT a legally valid DNR order — only official signed paperwork is.
🩺 If you have wishes about end-of-life care, put them in writing and tell your family.

If you were the doctor… would you follow the tattoo? 💬

#MedicalEthics #EmergencyMedicine #DNR
```

## 2. Her urine bag turned PURPLE 💜
📷 `02_purple_urine_bag.jpg` · Original: Plaçais & Denier, NEJM 2019 · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/purple-urine-bag-syndrome/)
**Hook B:** *"Nurses walked in and found this. It's real — and it has a name."*
```
Nurses found her urine bag like this. Bright PURPLE. 💜😳

An elderly woman in France had been in the hospital after a stroke.
She'd needed a urinary catheter for days.

10 days in, the bag and tubing started turning purple.

.
.
.

👉 It's called PURPLE URINE BAG SYNDROME — and the cause is fascinating:

1️⃣ Food contains tryptophan (an amino acid).
2️⃣ The gut and liver turn it into a chemical that ends up in the urine.
3️⃣ Bacteria living in the catheter break it down into two dyes: INDIGO (blue) + INDIRUBIN (red).
4️⃣ Blue + red = purple — especially when the urine is alkaline, like hers.

🤯 The bag and tubing literally get dyed from the inside.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: indigo is the same blue dye used to color jeans. 👖
🩺 The color itself is usually harmless — but it's a sign of bacteria in the catheter, so doctors check for infection and often change the catheter.

Nurses and doctors — have you ever seen this? 💬

#Nursing #ClinicalImages #DidYouKnow
📷 NEJM Images in Clinical Medicine / via MEDizzy
```

## 3. No, that's not a rock in his hand 🐍
📷 `03_rattlesnake_bite_hand.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/rattlesnake-bite/)
**Hook B:** *"This is what rattlesnake venom does to a hand."*
```
No, that's not a rock or a balloon in his palm.
That's his HAND — after a rattlesnake bite. 🐍😳

Rattlesnake venom is a cocktail of toxins.
Some destroy tissue. Some stop the blood from clotting. Some can even paralyze muscles.

Within hours of a bite, the area can swell massively — tight, shiny and purple — as fluid and blood leak out of damaged vessels.

.
.
.

👉 The treatment is ANTIVENOM — and the faster, the better.

🤯 Rattlesnakes can still bite by reflex even after they're dead. Never pick one up.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: kids are at greater risk because they get the same dose of venom in a much smaller body.
🩺 If bitten:
✅ Call emergency services, stay calm and still
✅ Remove rings and watches (swelling comes fast)
❌ Don't cut the wound, suck out venom, or use a tight tourniquet — these myths can make it worse.

Have you ever seen a rattlesnake in the wild? 🐍
```

## 4. He tattooed his eyeball GREEN. It went horribly wrong 👁️💚
📷 `04_eyeball_tattoo_gone_wrong.jpg` · Original: *American Journal of Ophthalmology Case Reports* · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/eyeball-tattoo-gone-wrong/)
**Hook B:** *"The ink was mixed with rubbing alcohol. Then injected into his eye."*
```
He paid a tattoo parlor to turn the white of his eye GREEN.
4 days later, he was in the hospital. 👁️💚

A 26-year-old man in Mexico got an eyeball tattoo.
The green ink was diluted with rubbing alcohol, water and glycerin… and injected under the surface of his eye.

Then: severe pain, light sensitivity, a swollen eyelid, and his vision getting worse.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: a serious infection of the tissues around the eye (orbital cellulitis) plus inflammation of the eye wall.

He needed IV antibiotics, steroids, and even a procedure to temporarily stitch his eyelid partly closed to protect the eye.

✅ After about 2 weeks, the swelling went down and his vision improved.
But the green ink spread into his lower eyelid — permanently. 💚

🤯 Eyeball tattoos are usually done by tattoo artists, not eye doctors — the needle can go straight through the eye.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: the possible complications include permanent blindness and even losing the eye.
🩺 Ophthalmologists strongly warn against it. Your eyes aren't a canvas.

Would you ever get an eyeball tattoo? 👀

#EyeHealth #TattooFail #Ophthalmology
```

## 5. This is what X-rays did to hands before anyone knew they were dangerous ☢️
📷 `05_xray_radiation_hand_1900s.jpg` · Historical photo (via MEDizzy) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/the-hand-showing-damage-from-radiation-exposure-back-in-1900s/)
**Hook B:** *"Thomas Edison's assistant tested X-ray tubes on his own hands."*
```
In the early 1900s, nobody knew X-rays were dangerous.
This is what they did to the hands of the people who worked with them. ☢️😳

This photo has been widely shared as the hand of Clarence Dally — a glassblower who worked for THOMAS EDISON.

Dally helped build Edison's X-ray equipment.
To test the tubes, he held his own hands in the beam. Day after day.

.
.
.

👉 By 1900, his hands and face had radiation burns.
Ulcers wouldn't heal. Skin grafts failed.
Doctors amputated his left hand… then 4 fingers of the right… then both arms.

It wasn't enough. He died of cancer in 1904. 💔

He's thought to be the first American to die from radiation experimentation.

🤯 Edison was so shaken he abandoned X-ray research and said: "Don't talk to me about X-rays, I am afraid of them."
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: today's X-rays use a tiny fraction of the radiation — a chest X-ray is roughly the same as 10 days of the natural background radiation we all get anyway.
🩺 That's why radiographers step behind a shield — every protection rule exists because of people like Dally.

Did you know the history of X-rays was this dark? 💬

#MedicalHistory #Radiology #ThomasEdison
```

```
These aren't whip marks. They're TENTACLE prints. 🪼😳

A 31-year-old man was stung by a box jellyfish on a beach on Koh Samui, Thailand.
The tentacles left these ladder-like burn tracks across his skin.

Doctors treated him with vinegar…
but his condition kept getting worse.

.
.
.

👉 He needed CPR and was put on a ventilator in intensive care.

Box jellyfish are among the most venomous animals on Earth.
Their tentacles fire thousands of microscopic harpoons, injecting venom that can attack the heart within minutes.

🤯 Some box jellyfish are almost invisible in the water.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: the "ladder" or "caterpillar track" pattern on the skin is a classic sign of a box jellyfish sting.
🩺 In box jellyfish areas: swim at patrolled beaches, wear a stinger suit in season, and pour VINEGAR (not fresh water) over the sting while calling emergency services.
```

## 7. A burn peeled off his skin, and his tattoo was still there 🎨
📷 `07_burn_reveals_tattoo_ink.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/epidermal-burn-of-hand-exposes-bright-colours-of-tattoo/)
**Hook B:** *"The top layer of skin burned away. The ink underneath was BRIGHTER."*
```
A burn took off the top layer of his skin.
The tattoo underneath? Still there — and even BRIGHTER. 🎨😳

Look at the middle of the rose: where the outer skin peeled away, the ink is vivid blue.

.
.
.

👉 Here's why:
Tattoo ink isn't in the top layer of your skin (the epidermis) — that layer constantly sheds and replaces itself.
The ink is injected DEEPER, into the dermis.

Normally, you see your tattoo through a thin "frosted glass" layer of skin.
Burn that layer away… and you see the raw ink directly. 🔍

🤯 Your immune cells try to eat the ink — but when they die, new cells swallow the same ink again. That's why tattoos last a lifetime.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: you shed about 30,000–40,000 skin cells every minute — and your tattoo doesn't budge.
🩺 Burns over tattoos still need proper burn care — cool running water for 20 minutes, then medical review if it blisters.

Do you have a tattoo? What's the story behind it? 💬

#Tattoo #Dermatology #DidYouKnow
```

## 8. His urine turned GREEN in the ICU 💚
📷 `08_green_urine.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-green-urine/)
**Hook B:** *"5 days in intensive care. Then the nurse saw this."*
```
5 days into his ICU stay, his urine turned GREEN. 💚😳

A 62-year-old man with severe lung disease was on a breathing machine.
His liver tests were normal. No infection.
So what was going on?

.
.
.

👉 It was the SEDATIVE keeping him asleep: PROPOFOL.

The liver breaks propofol down into chemicals called phenols — and when the kidneys flush them out, they can tint urine green.

✅ When the propofol was stopped, his urine went back to normal. It's harmless.

🤯 Other things that can turn urine green or blue: a dye called methylene blue, some antidepressants… and certain bacteria.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: beets can turn your urine pink, and asparagus can change how it smells (but not everyone can smell it!).
🩺 Unusual urine color that doesn't go away = tell your doctor. Usually harmless, but worth checking.

Ever had a food turn your pee a weird color? 😅
```

```
That's not a stray eyelash.
That hair is GROWING OUT of his eyeball. 👁️😳

A teenage boy had a yellowish lump on the edge of his eye, where the colored part meets the white.
It had been slowly getting bigger since childhood.

Look closely: a hair is sprouting straight out of it.

.
.
.

👉 It's a LIMBAL DERMOID — a benign growth he was born with.

Very early in the womb, a small piece of SKIN-type tissue ended up in the wrong place: on the eye.
So it grows what skin grows: fat, glands… and HAIR.

🤯 These growths can contain hair follicles, sweat glands, fat — and in rare cases even muscle or tooth-like tissue.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: they affect roughly 1 to 3 in every 10,000 people.
🩺 Small ones can be watched; bigger ones that affect vision or cause irritation can be removed surgically.
```

## 10. A "black henna" holiday tattoo left her scarred 🌿
📷 `10_black_henna_scars.jpg` · via MEDizzy (originally shared by @medicaltalks) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/extreme-case-of-henna-tattooing-dermatitis/)
**Hook B:** *"It was supposed to wash off in 2 weeks. Look at it now."*
```
It was a "temporary" henna tattoo from a holiday.
This is what it left behind. 🌿😳

A young woman got a black henna tattoo on vacation.
Days later it started itching… then erupted into painful blisters exactly in the shape of the design.

She needed treatment at a specialist burns unit — and was left with raised scars.

.
.
.

👉 Real henna is reddish-brown.
"BLACK henna" often contains a chemical called PPD (para-phenylenediamine) — a hair-dye chemical that is not allowed to be applied directly to the skin in many countries.

On skin, it can trigger a severe allergic reaction that burns the pattern INTO you.

🤯 The reaction usually appears 7–14 days later — often after you're already home.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: one reaction can make you allergic for LIFE — to hair dyes, some clothing dyes, and even certain medicines.
🩺 Before any henna: ask if it's natural. If it's jet-black, walk away — especially for kids. 🚫

Have you ever had a henna tattoo? 💬
```
