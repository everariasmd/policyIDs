## 1. "Something flew into my eye." It left larvae behind 🪰👁️
📷 `01_larvae_in_eye.jpg` · Original: NEJM (Abihaidar et al.) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/rare-infestation-of-oestrus-ovis-larvae-in-gardeners-eye/)
**Hook B:** *"His itchy eye had over a dozen tiny passengers."*

```
He felt something hit his eye while gardening. A few hours later, it was still itching… 👁️

The 53-year-old was working near a sheep and horse farm.
It felt like a bug had flown straight into his eye.
Hours later, the itch still wouldn't go away.

In the ER, the doctor looked under magnification and saw them moving…

.
.
.

👉 Over a DOZEN tiny fly larvae crawling on the surface of his eye. 😳

The culprit: the SHEEP BOT FLY. 🐑
It normally squirts its larvae into sheep's noses — but sometimes it mistakes a human eye for one.

✅ The larvae were removed one by one with tiny tweezers.
10 days later his eye was completely fine.

🩺 Something "flew into your eye" and the irritation won't stop? Don't rub it — get it examined.

Would you have guessed a fly could do THIS? 🤯

## 2. The rash that MOVES overnight 🐛
📷 `02_larva_currens_skin.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-larva-currens-in-strongyloides-hyperinfection-syndrome/)
**Hook B:** *"Doctors traced the rash with a pen. The next day it had moved."*

```
Doctors drew around this rash with a pen. 24 hours later, it had MOVED. 🖊️

Look at the bottom photos 👇
The purple outlines are where the red streaks were the day before.
Now they're somewhere else.

This 64-year-old man had cancer and had just started high-dose steroids.
4 days later: an itchy, snake-like rash spreading across his body… and diarrhea.

.
.
.

👉 WORMS. Moving under his skin. 🐛

It's called larva currens ("racing larva") — caused by Strongyloides, a tiny parasitic worm.
It can hide silently in the gut for DECADES.
But when the immune system is suppressed (like with steroids), it multiplies out of control — this is called hyperinfection, and it can be deadly.

✅ Treated with an antiparasitic drug (ivermectin), his rash and diarrhea went away.

🩺 Before starting long-term steroids, people from areas where Strongyloides occurs may need screening.

Did you know a worm could live in your body for decades unnoticed? 😳

#Parasites #Dermatology #InfectiousDisease

## 3. Everyone blamed the leftover noodles. They were wrong ⚠️
📷 `03_purpura_fulminans.jpg` · Original: NEJM (Case Records of the MGH, 2021) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/purpura-fulminans-teen-suffers-sepsis-after-eating-leftover-noodles/)
**Hook B:** *"This photo went viral as 'leftover food poisoning.' The real cause is scarier."*

```
You may have seen this story online: "Teen nearly dies after eating leftover noodles." 🍜

Here's what actually happened 👇

A 19-year-old ate leftover chicken, rice and lo mein.
About 20 hours later: vomiting, belly pain, chills.
Then a stiff neck, blurry vision, trouble breathing…
and purple patches spreading across his skin.

His blood pressure crashed. He needed a ventilator. 🏥

.
.
.

✅ Blood tests found the real cause: Neisseria meningitidis — MENINGOCOCCAL bacteria in his bloodstream.

Those purple patches are "purpura fulminans" — tiny clots blocking blood flow so the skin starts to die.

🚫 The noodles were a coincidence. Meningococcus spreads person to person through saliva and close contact — NOT through food.

🩺 Fever + stiff neck + a purple rash that doesn't fade when pressed = go to the ER NOW. Every hour matters.
💉 Vaccines protect against several types of meningococcus. Ask your doctor if you're up to date.

Had you heard the "leftover noodles" version of this story? 🤔

#Meningitis #Sepsis #Vaccines #MythBusting

```
Mid root canal, he coughed twice. Then the dentist realized a tool was missing. 🦷

Can you find it in this X-ray? 👀

.
.
.

👉 See the arrow? That thin white line is a 27 mm metal ENDODONTIC FILE — a sharp, needle-like tool used to clean inside teeth.

The 62-year-old had swallowed it. 😬

Doctors planned to fish it out with a scope… but by the time they were ready, it had already traveled deep into his intestines.

So they did the only thing left: wait… and take X-rays to track its journey. 📸

✅ It passed naturally — no surgery needed.

🩺 Fun fact: this is exactly why many dentists use a rubber sheet (a "dental dam") during root canals.

## 5. His eye filled with AIR BUBBLES 🫧
📷 `05_bubbly_eye.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-orbital-emphysema/)
**Hook B:** *"He fell on his face. The next day, his eye was full of bubbles."*

```
He fell on his face. The next day his eye looked "bubbly." 🫧

The 63-year-old came in with double vision and an eye that felt like it was crackling when touched.

Look at the white of his eye — those are AIR BUBBLES trapped under the surface.

.
.
.

👉 The CT (right) explained it: the fall had cracked the thin bone under his eye.
Air from his SINUS escaped through the fracture and got trapped around the eye. 💨

It's called orbital emphysema.
The pressure was squeezing his eye — his vision dropped to 20/400.

✅ Doctors released the trapped air, and his vision improved.

🩺 After a face injury, DON'T blow your nose hard — it can force air into the eye socket. Get checked if you see swelling, crackling or double vision.

Did you know blowing your nose could do this after a fracture? 😮

#Ophthalmology #Trauma #CTscan

## 6. Her palms were the first sign of lung cancer ✋
📷 `06_triple_palms.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/triple-palms-a-rare-skin-condition-in-a-smoker/)
**Hook B:** *"Her doctor looked at her hands and ordered a chest scan."*

```
Her palms changed. Her doctor immediately ordered a chest scan. ✋

A 59-year-old woman who smoked noticed her palms getting darker and thicker over 5 months.
Look at photo A — the skin looks velvety, the fingerprint lines exaggerated.

No pain. No other symptoms.

.
.
.

👉 The CT found a MASS in her right lung. 🫁
Biopsy: lung cancer, already spread to nearby lymph nodes.

This skin sign is called "tripe palms" — because it looks like the lining of a cow's stomach.
It's a rare paraneoplastic sign: the tumor releases substances that make the skin grow abnormally.

✅ After surgery and chemotherapy, her palms returned to normal (photo B).

🩺 Lesson: sudden, unexplained skin changes — especially in smokers — deserve a full check-up. Sometimes the skin speaks first.

Did you know your hands could reveal cancer? 😳

#LungCancer #Dermatology #QuitSmoking

## 7. A night of drinking, 3 hours asleep in the cold ⚠️
📷 `07_frostbite.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/frostbite-frozen-fingers/)
**Hook B:** *"-7°C. 3 hours. These were his fingers 2 days later."*

```
He drank too much and fell asleep outside. This is what -7°C did to his fingers. 🥶

The 24-year-old passed out in the cold for about 3 hours.
Two days later his fingers were swollen, purple-black, and completely numb.
A pinprick didn't even make them bleed.

👉 Diagnosis: FROSTBITE.
The tissue had literally frozen — ice crystals forming inside the cells. ❄️

Alcohol makes it worse: it widens blood vessels so you lose heat faster… while making you feel warm. 🍺

✅ The good news: with careful wound care, his fingers healed over time — no amputation.
But 2 years later, his hands still couldn't tolerate the cold.

🩺 Never let a drunk friend "sleep it off" outside in winter.

How cold does it get where you live? ❄️

#Frostbite #WinterSafety #EmergencyMedicine

## 8. Her tongue was dying, and so was her vision 👅
📷 `08_tongue_necrosis.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/tongue-necrosis-a-manifestation-of-giant-cell-arteritis/)
**Hook B:** *"A headache. A swollen tongue. Then she went blind in one eye."*

```
A headache. A swollen, painful tongue. Then she lost sight in one eye. 👁️

The 74-year-old's tongue had turned white and ulcerated — parts of it were DYING (photo A).
The artery at her temple was thick and tender.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: Giant Cell Arteritis.

It's an inflammation of the arteries in the head.
Swollen vessels choke off blood supply — to the tongue, the jaw… and the EYES.
Her tongue was literally starving for blood.

✅ With high-dose steroids and another immune drug, her vision came back and the tongue healed (photo B).

🩺 Over 50 with a NEW headache, scalp tenderness, jaw pain while chewing, or vision changes? This is an EMERGENCY — untreated, it can cause permanent blindness.

Had you ever heard of giant cell arteritis? 💬

## 9. The skin around her mouth was hiding stomach cancer
📷 `09_acanthosis_cancer.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-malignant-acanthosis-nigricans/)
**Hook B:** *"Velvety dark skin in her armpits. Warts around her lips. One cause."*

```
3 months of strange skin changes. The cause was in her stomach. 😟

A 30-year-old woman developed:
👉 rough, wart-like growths around her lips and nose (A)
👉 dark, velvety skin in her armpits, neck and groin (B)
👉 heartburn, fatigue… and 15 kg of weight loss she couldn't explain

.
.
.

✅ Diagnosis: Malignant acanthosis nigricans.

Common acanthosis nigricans (dark velvety skin) is usually linked to insulin resistance.
But when it appears SUDDENLY, spreads fast, and involves the lips and mouth — it can be a warning sign of hidden cancer.

The CT showed thickening of her stomach and a mass on her ovary (C, D).
Biopsy: stomach cancer that had spread to the ovary.

🩺 Lesson: rapid skin changes + unexplained weight loss = see a doctor. The skin can be a window to what's happening inside.

Did you know skin changes could be a sign of cancer? 💬

#Dermatology #Oncology #EarlyDetection

## 10. Her kidney MOVES when she stands up 🧐
📷 `10_floating_kidney.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/floating-kidney-nephroptosis/)
**Hook B:** *"'It feels like a ball rolling inside me.' 6 years, no diagnosis."*

```
"It feels like a ball rolling inside me." 6 years. Many doctors. No answer. 🧐

The 28-year-old had right-sided belly pain that got WORSE every time she stood up.
All her tests came back normal.

Then doctors took 2 X-rays with dye: one lying down, one standing.

Compare the right kidney (left side of each image) 👀

.
.
.

👉 Lying down: kidney in its normal place.
👉 Standing up: it DROPS down toward her pelvis.

✅ Diagnosis: Nephroptosis — "floating kidney."
The kidney isn't anchored properly, so it slides down when she's upright, kinking its vessels and tube.

A minor operation stitched it into place (nephropexy) — and she went home with no complications.

🩺 Lesson: when pain depends on POSITION, test the patient in that position.

Have you ever had symptoms that doctors just couldn't figure out? 💬
