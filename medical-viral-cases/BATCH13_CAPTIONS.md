## 1. Surgeons opened this tumor and found HAIR and TEETH 🦷
📷 `01_tumor_with_hair_and_teeth.jpg` · Case reported by the Japanese Society of Neuropathology (via MEDizzy) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/cystic-ovarian-masses-with-well-differentiated-brain-and-brainstem-tissue/)
**Hook B:** *"Hair. Sometimes teeth. And in one case — a tiny piece of BRAIN."*
```
Surgeons cut open this tumor from a young woman's ovary.
Inside: tangles of HAIR, grease, and hard lumps. 😳
Tumors like this can even grow TEETH and bone.

It's called a TERATOMA — from the Greek for "monster tumor."

.
.
.

👉 Teratomas grow from special cells (germ cells) that can turn into ANY type of tissue in the body.
When those cells grow out of control, they build random body parts: hair, skin, teeth, bone, fat… even thyroid tissue.

In one case reported by Japanese doctors, a teenage girl's ovarian teratoma contained organized BRAIN tissue — with structures resembling a cerebellum and brainstem, surrounded by a thin shell of bone like a mini skull. 🧠

🤯 Most ovarian teratomas are benign — and they're one of the most common ovarian tumors in young women.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: they're also called "dermoid cysts."
🩺 Many are found by accident on a scan. Pelvic pain or a feeling of fullness that doesn't go away = get an ultrasound.

Had you ever heard of a tumor that grows teeth? 💬

#Teratoma #WomensHealth #WeirdButTrue

## 3. "It's just a fungus." It was CANCER
📷 `03_mycosis_fungoides_before_after.jpg` · Image: NIH (via MEDizzy) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/cancer-or-fungus/)
**Hook B:** *"Same man. Before and after just 2 rounds of treatment."*
```
Same man. LEFT: before treatment. RIGHT: after just 2 rounds. 😳

A 53-year-old had lived with skin bumps for 7 years.
Then, over 2 months, lumps grew all over his face and neck — distorting his features.
85% of his skin was affected.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: MYCOSIS FUNGOIDES.

Despite the name, it's NOT a fungus.
It's a CANCER — a lymphoma of T-cells (a type of white blood cell) that attacks the skin first.
It's called "fungoides" because the lumps can look like mushrooms. 🍄

In his case it had spread to his lymph nodes, spleen and liver.

✅ After 2 cycles of chemotherapy and targeted therapy, the change was dramatic (right photo). 🙌

🤯 It often starts as itchy, scaly patches that look like eczema or psoriasis — for YEARS.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: it's the most common type of lymphoma that starts in the skin.
🩺 A "rash" that won't go away with usual creams for months = ask for a skin biopsy.

Would you have guessed this was cancer? 💬

#Lymphoma #Dermatology #CancerAwareness

## 4. 7 hours with paint thinner, no gloves 🧤
📷 `04_paint_thinner_hands.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/chemical-burn-while-handling-a-paint-thinner/)
**Hook B:** *"His fingers look like leather. This was a chemical, not fire."*
```
No fire touched these hands.
This was done by PAINT THINNER. 🧤😳

A 33-year-old man worked with a strong paint stripper for about 7 HOURS — without gloves.

His palms and fingers started burning intensely.
Look at the result: dark, waxy, wrinkled skin… his fingertips look like LEATHER.

.
.
.

👉 It's a CHEMICAL BURN.

Solvents like this dissolve the fats in your skin and cell walls.
Unlike a heat burn, the damage keeps going as long as the chemical stays on your skin.

🤯 Chemical burns can look mild at first and get worse over hours.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: for most chemical burns, rinsing with running water for a LONG time (20+ minutes) is the first and most important treatment.
🩺 Wear chemical-resistant gloves, work in a ventilated space, and read the label. If exposed: remove contaminated clothing/jewelry and rinse immediately.

Be honest — do you wear gloves for DIY jobs? 🛠️

## 5. She kept the same IUD for 20 YEARS
📷 `05_iud_left_20_years.jpg` · Original: NEJM (Arakaki et al.) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/woman-suffers-with-actinomycosis-after-leaving-iud-in-for-20-years/)
**Hook B:** *"It was supposed to be replaced after 5 years."*
```
Her IUD should have been replaced after 5 years.
She kept it for 20. 😳

A 54-year-old woman had months of weight loss, fever, pain — and trouble walking.
Doctors felt a lump in her lower belly.

The CT scan showed pockets of infection (abscesses) around her IUD (right photo, after removal).

.
.
.

👉 The bacteria responsible, Actinomyces, normally lives harmlessly in the body.
But with an old plastic IUD left in place for decades, it grew into a serious infection — spreading through her pelvis and into her HIP JOINT.

✅ After the IUD was removed and a course of antibiotics, she made a full recovery.

🤯 The study authors noted pelvic actinomycosis is linked to long-term IUD use — the longer it stays in, the higher the risk.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: different IUDs last different lengths of time — some about 3 years, others 10 years or more.
🩺 Know WHEN your IUD was inserted and WHEN it expires. Put a reminder in your phone. 📱

Ladies — do you know when your IUD expires? 💬

#WomensHealth #Contraception #IUD

## 6. Her cat's claw left a GLOWING scratch on her eye 🐈
📷 `06_glowing_cat_scratch_eye.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/cat-endangers-vision-of-a-10-year-old-girl/)
**Hook B:** *"The glowing green line is a cut on her eye."*
```
That glowing green line is a CUT across a 10-year-old's eye. 🐈😳

She was playing with her cat when its claw caught her eye.
At first she told her mom she fell. Mom gave her an ice pack.

But within hours, the pain was unbearable, her eye was streaming, and her vision went blurry.

.
.
.

👉 In the ER, doctors put a yellow dye (fluorescein) in her eye and shone a blue light.
Any damage to the clear front of the eye (the cornea) GLOWS green. ✨

The cat's claw had sliced her cornea.

✅ She started antibiotic eye drops right away and got a tetanus booster. 3 days later, her eye was much better.

🤯 This dye test is how doctors find scratches invisible to the naked eye.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: the cornea is one of the most nerve-dense tissues in the body — that's why even a tiny scratch hurts so much.
🩺 Any eye injury from an animal claw, branch or fingernail = see a doctor the same day. Don't rub it.

Cat owners — has your cat ever scratched you somewhere scary? 🐾

## 8. He used to catch ARMADILLOS. 30 years later, he got leprosy 🦔
📷 `08_leprosy_armadillo_hunter.jpg` · Original: Logas & Holloway, BMJ Case Reports 2019 · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/doctors-baffled-with-lesion-on-armadillo-hunters-hand/)
**Hook B:** *"Doctors were stumped — until he mentioned his old job."*
```
For 5 months, this mark on his arm kept growing.
It hurt sometimes — and the skin felt NUMB. 😳

The 58-year-old Florida man was tested for Lyme disease (negative) and given antihistamines. It got worse.

Doctors were stumped… until they asked about his past.

.
.
.

👉 30 years earlier, he'd been an ARMADILLO HUNTER — trapping them to sell to researchers.

Diagnosis: LEPROSY (Hansen's disease).

In the southern US, armadillos can carry the leprosy bacteria — and pass it to people who handle them.
Leprosy can take YEARS to show symptoms after exposure.

✅ He started a course of antibiotics. 5 months later, the patch had shrunk a lot — and feeling was returning. 🙌

🤯 Leprosy is fully curable with antibiotics today — and most people are naturally resistant to it.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: armadillos are one of very few animals known to naturally carry leprosy.
🩺 A skin patch with NUMBNESS that won't go away = see a doctor, and mention any animal contact.

Did you know armadillos could carry leprosy? 💬

## 9. Try this: can you press your palms flat together? 🙏
📷 `09_diabetes_stiff_hands.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/know-what-uncontrolled-diabetes-mellitus-can-do/)
**Hook B:** *"She couldn't lay her hands flat on a table. The cause was her blood sugar."*
```
Put your hands together like you're praying, fingers spread. Can your palms and fingers touch completely? 🙏

This 50-year-old woman couldn't.
Her hands had been stiff and painful for a YEAR. She couldn't fully bend or straighten her fingers.
The skin on her hands had turned thick, tight and WAXY.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: DIABETIC CHEIROARTHROPATHY — "stiff hand syndrome."

She'd had diabetes for 10 years, but skipped check-ups for a year. Her blood sugar was way out of control (HbA1c 10%).
High sugar sticks to collagen in the skin and tendons, making them stiff — like caramelizing.

Further tests found diabetes was ALSO damaging her kidneys and eyes.

🤯 Doctors use two quick tests: the "prayer sign" (palms can't meet) and the "tabletop sign" (hand can't lie flat on a table).
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: your hands can be an early warning of diabetes damage elsewhere in the body.
🩺 Diabetes check-ups matter even when you feel fine. Good sugar control helps prevent this.

Did you try the prayer test? Did your palms touch? 👀

## 10. He popped his ears so often his eardrum ballooned OUT 🎈👂
📷 `10_ballooning_eardrum.jpg` · Original: BMJ Case Reports · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/ballooning-herniation-of-tympanic-membrane/)
**Hook B:** *"That round bubble inside his ear canal? It's his eardrum."*
```
That round bubble inside his ear canal? It's his EARDRUM — ballooning outward. 🎈👂

A 30-year-old officer and water-sports lover had 10 years of ear infections.
He'd learned that "popping" his ears (holding his nose and blowing) made his hearing better.

So he did it… a LOT.

Then doctors found a soft, round mass filling his ear canal.

.
.
.

👉 It was his eardrum, stretched out of place — a rare "ballooning herniation."

Years of damage from infections had weakened it.
Every forceful pop pushed air against the weak spot… blowing it out like a balloon.

🤯 The doctors noted the irony: the ear-popping that relieved his symptoms was also part of what caused the problem.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: gentle swallowing or yawning is usually enough to equalize ear pressure — no need to force it.
🩺 Frequent ear infections or a blocked ear that needs constant popping = see an ENT doctor.

Do you pop your ears a lot? 👂

#ENT #EarHealth #DidYouKnow
