# Viral-style captions (v2): replaces the captions in POSTS.md & MEDIZZY_POSTS.md

## The playbook these captions follow

**1. Win the first line.** On mobile, Facebook shows roughly the first 90 to 125 characters, then "See more". The hook must stand alone and **create a question without answering it**. Every caption below opens with a hook under ~90 characters.

**2. The 5 hook styles that dominate viral medical pages**
| Style | Template | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 🕵️ Mystery | "He came in for X. Doctors found Y." | Curiosity gap |
| 🚫 Denial | "This is NOT a tattoo." | Pattern break, and the brain wants the real answer |
| 🧩 Quiz | "Only doctors get this right in 5 seconds." | Ego plus a challenge, which brings comments |
| 😱 Number shock | "2 kg. 20 cm. Inside a man." | Concrete specifics feel real |
| 🧠 Myth bust | "Everyone thinks it's from pork. It's not." | People love being corrected, and they share it |

**3. Structure:** Hook → (blank line) → story in 3 to 6 **short lines** → twist or diagnosis → 1 lesson → **one specific question**.

**4. Hide the answer.** Put the diagnosis below a row of dots (`.` on separate lines) or in the **first comment**. People open comments to find it, which tells the algorithm the post is interesting.

**5. ❌ Never use engagement bait.** Facebook demotes posts (and repeat-offender Pages) that say "Tag a friend", "Share if…", "Comment YES", "Like if…". Instead, ask a **genuine, specific question** ("What would you have guessed?", "Have you ever seen this in practice?"). That gets more comments AND avoids the penalty. (I removed the "Tag someone" lines from the v1 captions.)

**6. Saves and shares come from usefulness.** End with one practical takeaway ("If you have X, see a doctor").

**7. Light formatting:** 3 to 6 emojis max, 2 to 4 hashtags at the end, credit line last.

**8. A/B test:** each post has a **Hook A** and **Hook B**. Post A; repost B about 6 weeks later and keep the winner's style.

## Posting tips
- **Comment the answer yourself** on quiz posts (#4, #9) about 2 hours after posting, and **pin it**.
- **Reply to the first 10 to 20 comments within the first hour.** Replies count as comments and keep the post in feeds.
- **Best slots:** test evenings (7 to 10 pm in your audience's main time zone) vs. lunchtime, and keep the winner.
- **Lead with the image**, never a link. Put source links in the first comment.
- **Track in Meta Business Suite:** shares and comments per reach matter more than likes. Repeat the hook *styles* that win.
