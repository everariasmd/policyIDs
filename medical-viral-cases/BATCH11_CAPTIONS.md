## 1. His tongue swelled out of his mouth in 5 hours 👅
📷 `01_giant_swollen_tongue.jpg` · Case (Hirschy et al., *Adv Emerg Nurs J* 2018) via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/acutely-swollen-tongue-in-71-year-old-man/)
**Hook B:** *"It wasn't an allergy. It was his blood pressure pill."*
```
In just 5 hours, his tongue swelled so big it wouldn't fit in his mouth. 👅😳

A 71-year-old man rushed to the ER.
No rash. No hives. No bee sting. No new food. No new medicine.

Doctors gave him adrenaline (epinephrine) — the classic allergy treatment.
It didn't work.

.
.
.

👉 The cause was a blood pressure pill he'd been taking: ENALAPRIL, an "ACE inhibitor."

These drugs can cause a rare reaction called ANGIOEDEMA — deep swelling of the tongue, lips or throat.
It's not driven by histamine like a normal allergy, which is why allergy treatments often don't help.

Doctors had to put a breathing tube in while he was AWAKE, before the swelling closed his airway.
✅ They stopped the pill. By the next day the swelling was gone, and the tube came out.

🤯 This reaction can appear years after someone starts the medicine — not just in the first days.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: ACE inhibitor names usually end in "-pril" (enalapril, lisinopril, ramipril).
🩺 On a "-pril" medicine and your lips, tongue or throat start to swell? Call emergency services immediately.

Do you or someone you know take a "-pril" medicine? 💊

#Angioedema #BloodPressure #EmergencyMedicine

## 2. You can WRITE on her skin ✍️
📷 `02_skin_writing_dermographism.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/skin-writing/)
**Hook B:** *"Every line on this back was drawn with just a fingernail."*
```
Nobody drew on this back with a marker.
Every line appeared just from lightly SCRATCHING the skin. ✍️😳

Within minutes, wherever the skin is stroked, it rises into raised red-and-white welts — in the exact shape of the scratch.

.
.
.

👉 It's called DERMOGRAPHISM — literally "skin writing."

In these people, immune cells in the skin (mast cells) are extra sensitive.
Even light pressure makes them release chemicals that cause local swelling — tracing whatever you "wrote."
The marks usually fade within 30 minutes.

🤯 Some people with it have used their skin as a temporary canvas — writing names or drawing patterns.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: it affects about 5% of people — 1 in 20. Many have no idea it has a name.
🩺 It's usually harmless. If it's itchy or bothersome, antihistamines often help. Avoid scratching and tight clothes.

Try it: lightly scratch your arm with a fingernail. Did a raised line appear? 👀

#Dermographism #Dermatology #DidYouKnow

## 3. A floss pick went down the wrong way, and he found out 8 YEARS later 🦷
📷 `03_floss_pick_in_lung_8yrs.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/dental-floss-pick-impacted-in-the-windpipe-for-8-years/)
**Hook B:** *"After a sneeze, he started coughing blood. The cause: 8 years old."*
```
This dental floss pick was pulled out of a man's LUNG.
It had been there for 8 YEARS. 🦷😳

A healthy 32-year-old sneezed hard… and started coughing up blood.
No smoking. No lung disease. No bleeding problems.

The CT scan showed something long and needle-shaped stuck inside his left main airway.

.
.
.

👉 Doctors went in with a camera and pulled out THIS: a plastic floss pick.

Only afterwards did he remember: about 8 years earlier, he'd accidentally swallowed one — it had gone down the wrong pipe. He never had a single symptom… until that sneeze.

✅ After it was removed, the bleeding stopped and he went home the same day.

🤯 The body had grown tissue around it — that's how it stayed hidden for so long.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: inhaled objects most often end up in the RIGHT lung (that airway is wider and straighter) — this one went left.
🩺 Ever choked on something and then "it went away"? If you get a new cough, wheezing or cough up blood, tell your doctor about it.

Do you floss every day? Be honest. 😅

## 4. He was born with "angel wings" on his back 👼
📷 `04_angel_wings_birthmark.jpg` · via MEDizzy (originally shared by his family) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/boy-with-a-skin-condition-resembling-a-pair-of-wings/)
**Hook B:** *"His mother says he's one of the lucky ones. Here's why."*
```
This little boy was born with a pair of "wings." 👼

The giant birthmark stretches across his upper back, shaped almost perfectly like feathered angel wings.

His mother says he's "one of the fortunate ones" — some children with this condition have marks covering much more of their body, even their face.

.
.
.

👉 It's a GIANT CONGENITAL MELANOCYTIC NEVUS — a huge mole present from birth.

It happens in roughly 1 in 20,000 babies.
These moles grow as the child grows, and can get darker, hairier or bumpier around puberty.

🤯 It's not just skin-deep: doctors also do MRI scans because rarely these birthmarks are linked to changes in the brain or spine.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: giant congenital moles carry a higher risk of melanoma — so he has check-ups every few months.
🩺 Any birthmark that changes shape, color or size, bleeds or itches = show a doctor.

What do you see — wings? 😊💙

## 5. Her cat gave her a POX infection in her eye 🐈
📷 `05_cowpox_eye_from_cat.jpg` · Original: NEJM (Royal Free Hospital, London) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/woman-at-risk-of-becoming-blind-after-contracting-cowpox-infection-from-pet-cat/)
**Hook B:** *"Her cat had sores on its paws. Two weeks later, her eye looked like this."*
```
Two weeks before her eye got infected, her CAT had sores on its paws. 🐈😳

A 28-year-old woman in the UK had a red, irritated eye with discharge.
Antibiotic drops didn't help. Antiviral drops didn't help.
It got worse — spreading into the tissues around her eye.
Doctors feared she could lose her sight.

Then she mentioned her cat (photo C)…

.
.
.

👉 Both the cat and the woman tested positive for COWPOX — a cousin of the smallpox virus. 😳

Today cowpox lives mostly in wild rodents. Cats catch it while hunting… and pass it to their owners.
She probably got it by petting her cat, then touching her eye.

✅ After a special antiviral drug and surgery, 6 months later she had perfect 20/20 vision — with a slightly droopy eyelid.

🤯 Cowpox is how vaccines were invented: in 1796, Edward Jenner used it to protect people from smallpox.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: the word "vaccine" comes from "vacca," Latin for COW. 🐄
🩺 If your pet has unusual sores, see a vet — and wash your hands before touching your face.

Cat owners — does your cat hunt mice? 🐭

#CatOwners #Zoonosis #EyeHealth

## 6. This baby was BORN with a tooth 👶🦷

**Hook B:** *"Her mother noticed something sharp while breastfeeding."*
```
She was only 5 days old — and she already had a TOOTH. 👶🦷

Her mother noticed it right away: a small white tooth in the front of her lower gum.
Breastfeeding was painful.

Her parents brought her to a pediatric dentist.

.
.
.

👉 It's called a NATAL TOOTH — a tooth already present at birth.

This one was wobbly, with no proper root.
Loose natal teeth can be a choking risk if they fall out, and they can make feeding painful.

✅ After a vitamin K injection (to help with clotting in newborns), the dentist gently removed it with only a numbing gel. Baby and mom did great. 💕

🤯 Natal teeth happen in roughly 1 in 2,000–3,000 births.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: history says some famous people were born with teeth — including, reportedly, Napoleon and King Louis XIV.
🩺 Most natal teeth are harmless, but always have a dentist check — some are left in, some are removed.

## 7. His skull looked like raindrops hitting water 🌧️
📷 `07_raindrop_skull.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/skull-showing-a-pattern-of-raindrops-hitting-a-surface-and-splashing/)
**Hook B:** *"Doctors call this the 'raindrop skull.' Each dark spot is a hole."*
```
Every dark spot on this skull is a HOLE in the bone. 🌧️😳

Doctors call it the "raindrop skull" — it looks like rain splashing on a surface.

A 46-year-old man came in exhausted, short of breath, with back pain.
He'd lost 30 kg in 10 months.
His blood was dangerously low (hemoglobin 4.9 — normal is around 13+), his kidneys were failing, and his calcium was high. He also had a broken pelvic bone.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: MULTIPLE MYELOMA — a cancer of the blood's plasma cells.

The cancer cells crowd the bone marrow and release substances that dissolve bone from the inside — punching holes like these.

🤯 He had all four classic warning signs, which doctors remember as "CRAB": high Calcium, Renal (kidney) failure, Anemia, and Bone damage.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: myeloma is the second most common blood cancer.
🩺 Unexplained back/bone pain + tiredness + weight loss = ask for blood tests. Don't ignore it.

He started treatment, including blood transfusions.

Have you ever heard of multiple myeloma? 💬

## 8. A copper ring formed in her eyes 👁️🟤
📷 `08_copper_ring_in_eye.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/kayser-fleischer-ring-with-wilsons-disease/)
**Hook B:** *"Look at the edge of her iris. That brown ring is METAL."*
```
See the golden-brown ring around the edge of her iris (top photo)?
That's COPPER. Building up inside her eye. 👁️😳

A 17-year-old girl had Wilson's disease — a genetic condition where the body can't get rid of copper.

Copper from normal food slowly piles up in the liver, the brain… and the eyes.

.
.
.

👉 This is a KAYSER–FLEISCHER RING — a classic sign of Wilson's disease, sometimes visible to the naked eye.

Without treatment, copper buildup can cause liver failure, tremors, trouble walking and speaking, and even personality changes.

✅ After years of medicine that pulls copper out of the body, the ring completely disappeared (bottom photo). ✨

🤯 It's one of the few diseases doctors can sometimes suspect just by looking into your eyes.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: foods highest in copper include shellfish, nuts, chocolate and liver — people with Wilson's limit them.
🩺 A young person with unexplained liver problems or tremors should be tested for Wilson's — it's very treatable if caught.

Did you know your eyes could show copper poisoning? 💬

## 9. His blood vessels connect the WRONG way
📷 `09_parkes_weber_hands.jpg` · Image credit: Mohan, Varghese, Kumar & Subramanian (Govt. Dental College, Kottayam), via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-a-patient-suffering-from-parks-weber-syndrome/)
**Hook B:** *"Swollen, twisted and covered in bulging veins. He was born with this."*
```
Look at these hands. The swelling, the bulging veins, the purple patches.
He was born with the cause. 😳

Normally, blood flows: arteries ➡️ tiny capillaries ➡️ veins.

In this condition, some arteries connect DIRECTLY to veins — skipping the capillaries.
High-pressure blood rushes straight into the veins…

.
.
.

👉 It's PARKES WEBER SYNDROME — a rare condition present from birth.

✔️ "Port-wine" stains on the skin
✔️ Dozens of tiny artery-to-vein shortcuts (fistulas)
✔️ Overgrowth of the affected limb — the extra blood flow makes it grow bigger

🤯 All that extra blood flow can make the heart work so hard it eventually starts to fail.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: port-wine stains get their name because they look like spilled red wine.
🩺 A limb that's bigger, warmer or has a "buzzing" feel over a birthmark should be checked by a vascular specialist.

Had you ever heard of this condition? 💬

## 10. Chickenpox spread to his LUNGS ⚠️
📷 `10_deadly_chickenpox.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/disseminated-varicella-infection-in-an-immunocompromised-patient/)
⚠️ *The patient died. Frame it as a protective message.*
**Hook B:** *"Most people think chickenpox is a harmless kids' illness. Not for him."*
```
Most people think chickenpox is a harmless childhood illness.
For this 29-year-old, it was deadly. 💔

He had a bone marrow disease and was taking medicine that weakens the immune system.
Then he was around someone with chickenpox.

Days later, an itchy rash covered his face, chest and arms.
Then fever, cough… and he couldn't breathe.

His chest X-ray (right): both lungs full of spots of infection.

.
.
.

👉 DISSEMINATED VARICELLA — the chickenpox virus had spread beyond his skin into his lungs and liver.

Doctors gave IV antiviral drugs and put him on a ventilator.
Sadly, he died 2 days after arriving at the hospital.

🤯 In people with weak immune systems, the death rate from this form can be over 50%.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: the same virus hides in your nerves for life — and can come back decades later as SHINGLES.
🩺 Vaccines protect not just you, but people who CAN'T fight infections — cancer patients, transplant patients, newborns. 💉

Did you have chickenpox as a kid? 💬
