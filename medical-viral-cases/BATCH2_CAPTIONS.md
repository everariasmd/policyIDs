## 2. The tiny battery that nearly killed a baby 🔋
📷 `02_button_battery.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/button-battery-ingestion-in-11-month-old/)
**Hook B:** *"It looks like a coin on the X-ray. It's far more dangerous."*

```
Parents: please read this one. 🔋⚠️

An 11-month-old girl had a cough and trouble swallowing for 2 WEEKS.
Nobody knew why.

Then the X-ray 👇
A round object stuck in her throat — with a faint "double ring" edge.
That ring is how doctors tell a BUTTON BATTERY apart from a coin.

It was removed right away. It looked like she'd be fine.

10 hours later, she started vomiting massive amounts of blood. 🩸
The battery had burned a hole between her esophagus and a major artery in her neck.

Emergency surgery saved her life.
✅ She went home 32 days later with no lasting damage.

🧠 Why so dangerous? A button battery stuck in the throat reacts with moisture and can start burning tissue within hours.

👉 If you think a child swallowed one: go to the ER immediately. Don't wait for symptoms.

How many devices in your home use these batteries? Check the battery covers tonight. 🔍

#ChildSafety #ButtonBattery #Pediatrics #Radiology
```

## 3. Her intestines lit up on the X-ray, and it was Pepto-Bismol 💗
📷 `03_pepto_bismol_xray.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/bismuth-subsalicylate-poisoning/)
**Hook B:** *"Your stomach medicine shows up on X-rays like METAL."*

```
Why is this woman's entire bowel glowing white on the X-ray? 🤔

No dye. No contrast. Nothing swallowed by mistake.

She was 70, and had been battling bad diarrhea.
So she kept drinking more and more of a very common pink stomach medicine… 💗

.
.
.

👉 Bismuth subsalicylate — the active ingredient in Pepto-Bismol-type medicines.

Bismuth is so dense it shows up on X-ray almost like LEAD.
And the "salicylate" part? It's a cousin of aspirin. 💊

She arrived confused, vomiting and short of breath, with toxic salicylate levels in her blood.
✅ She recovered after IV treatment and dialysis.

🩺 Lesson: "over-the-counter" doesn't mean "unlimited." Always follow the dose on the label.

Did you know your stomach medicine contains an aspirin-like drug? 😮

#Radiology #Pharmacology #MedicalCases
```

## 4. Six white stripes on his nails tell a story 💅
📷 `04_mees_lines_nails.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/transverse-leukonychia-mees-lines/)
**Hook B:** *"Count the stripes. Each one is a round of chemo."*

```
Count the white stripes on his nails. Now guess what each one means. 🤔

He's 30.
These lines appeared over 4 months — first one, then another, then another…

.
.
.

👉 Each stripe = one round of CHEMOTHERAPY. 💉

He was being treated for lymphoma, and received 6 cycles.
Every cycle briefly disrupted nail growth — leaving a white band behind.

These are called Mees' lines (transverse leukonychia).
Your nails are like a diary of what happened to your body. 📖

✅ They grow out over time as the nail grows.

🩺 New lines on your nails after an illness or medication? Mention it to your doctor — nails can reveal a lot.

Have you ever noticed lines on your nails after being sick? 💬

#Dermatology #Oncology #NailHealth
```

## 5. His shoe size went up 3 sizes in 10 years 👣
📷 `05_acromegaly.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-34-year-old-with-acromegaly/)
**Hook B:** *"Same man. 15 years apart. Doctors spotted it in seconds."*

```
Same man. 15 years apart. Can you see what changed? 👀

At 34, he came to the ER with dangerously high blood sugar.
But the doctors noticed something else — his FACE.

A broader nose. A heavier brow. A jaw that pushed forward.
He also mentioned his shoe size had gone up 3 sizes over 10 years… and his vision was getting blurry.

.
.
.

👉 Acromegaly.

An MRI found a tumor on his pituitary gland — about 6 cm — pumping out huge amounts of GROWTH HORMONE. 🧠
In adults, bones can't grow longer, so they grow THICKER: hands, feet, face, jaw.
And the tumor was pressing on the nerves for his vision.

✅ Surgeons removed the tumor, and he continues treatment to control it.

🩺 Changes this slow are easy to miss in the mirror. Old photos can reveal them.

Would you have noticed this in yourself? 🤔
```

## 6. His gums told doctors where he worked 🦷
📷 `06_lead_poisoning.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-lead-poisoning-in-47-year-old/)
**Hook B:** *"A dark line on the gums. A hand that wouldn't lift. One job."*

```
Look closely at his gums. That dark line is a clue. 🦷

A 47-year-old man had:
👉 belly pain and constipation for 3 years
👉 confusion for over a year
👉 a darkening tongue and gums
👉 and for 6 months… he couldn't lift his wrists (photo C)

His job? 5 years making car batteries. 🔋

.
.
.

✅ Diagnosis: LEAD POISONING.

That blue-black "lead line" on the gums is a classic sign.
Lead also damages nerves — causing "wrist drop."
His blood lead level was 8 TIMES the reference limit.

He stopped working at the factory and started chelation therapy.
6 months later, his belly and nerve symptoms had gone away — but the gum staining remained.

🩺 Lesson: always ask patients what they do for work. The job is often the diagnosis.

What job do you think carries the biggest hidden health risk? 💬

#OccupationalHealth #Toxicology #LeadPoisoning
```

## 7. Bloody eyes after falling into a canal 👁️
📷 `07_leptospirosis_eyes.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/subconjunctival-hemorrhage-in-leptospirosis/)
**Hook B:** *"He fell into a canal. 3 weeks later, his eyes looked like this."*

```
3 weeks after falling into a canal, his eyes looked like this. 👁️🩸

18 years old. Previously healthy.
Then: fever, vomiting, diarrhea.

In the ER doctors found:
🔴 Bleeding in the whites of BOTH eyes
🟡 Yellow eyes (jaundice)
⚠️ His kidneys and liver starting to fail

The clue was in his story: that canal water. 💧

.
.
.

✅ Diagnosis: LEPTOSPIROSIS (Weil's disease).

It's caused by bacteria spread through the URINE of infected animals — like rats and mice — that contaminates water and soil.
The bacteria enter through cuts or through the eyes and mouth.

He was treated with IV penicillin and fully recovered within 3 weeks. 🙌

🩺 Fever after floods, swimming in fresh water, or contact with rats? Tell your doctor — this is treatable if caught early.

Did you know rodent urine in water could do this? 🐀

#InfectiousDisease #Leptospirosis #Ophthalmology
```

## 8. What's growing on his armpit hair? 🤢
📷 `08_trichomycosis_armpit.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/trichomycosis-axillaris/)
**Hook B:** *"It glows under UV light. And it's growing on his armpit hair."*

```
It's not dandruff. It's not deodorant. And it GLOWS under UV light. 💡

A 35-year-old man noticed white clumps stuck to his armpit hair for 9 MONTHS.
Washing with antibacterial soap didn't remove them.
Shaving once didn't fix it either.

Under a UV (Wood's) lamp, the hair lit up. ✨
Under the microscope: cotton-like crusts coating every strand.

.
.
.

✅ Diagnosis: Trichomycosis axillaris.

The name says "fungus" — but it's actually BACTERIA (Corynebacterium) feeding on dried sweat and sticking to the hair. 🦠

It's harmless and very common in people who sweat a lot.
One week of an antibiotic gel + benzoyl peroxide wash cleared it completely.

Be honest — have you ever checked your armpit hair this closely? 😂

#Dermatology #SkinHealth #MedicalCases
```

## 9. A ball of fungus growing inside his lung 🍄
📷 `09_aspergilloma_lung.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/case-of-pulmonary-aspergillloma/)
**Hook B:** *"The TB was cured. Then something moved into the hole it left."*

```
His tuberculosis was cured. But it left a hole in his lung… and something moved in. 🍄

A 51-year-old man started coughing up blood.
16 months earlier, a CT scan showed an empty cavity in his right lung — damage from old TB (top image).

The new scan (bottom image)?
That empty hole now had a BALL inside it. ⚪

.
.
.

✅ Diagnosis: Aspergilloma — a "fungus ball."

The mold Aspergillus (its spores are in the air we all breathe) settled into the old cavity and grew into a solid mass.
In the lab it grew into the fuzzy grey colony on the right. 🧫

Fungus balls can erode blood vessels — which is why he was coughing blood.
He later needed a procedure to block the bleeding artery.

🩺 Lesson: lungs damaged by TB need long-term follow-up — even after the "cure."

Did you know mold could grow INSIDE a human lung? 😳

#Pulmonology #Radiology #CTscan
```

## 10. Her tongue was trembling on its own 👅
📷 `10_tongue_als.jpg` · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/tongue-atrophy-and-fasiculations/)
**Hook B:** *"Look at the edges of her tongue. Neurologists know this sign."*

```
Look at the edges of this tongue. Neurologists dread seeing this. 👅

She's only 32.
For 3 years she'd been getting weaker: dropping things, tripping, then needing help with daily life.

On examination, her tongue was:
👉 shrunken, with scalloped edges
👉 constantly rippling with tiny twitches (fasciculations)

That combination means the nerves that control speech and swallowing are dying.

.
.
.

Doctors' leading diagnosis: ALS (motor neuron disease) 💔

ALS slowly destroys the nerve cells that move our muscles — eventually affecting speaking, swallowing and breathing. There is no cure yet, but treatment and support can help with symptoms and quality of life.

🩺 Persistent muscle twitching WITH progressive weakness and muscle loss needs a neurologist. (Twitching alone is very common and usually harmless.)

💜 Sending strength to every family living with ALS. Has ALS touched your life?

#ALS #Neurology #MotorNeuronDisease
```
