## 1. These hands are full of CRYSTALS 💎
📷 `01_gout_hands.jpg` · Image: Medscape (via MEDizzy) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/gouty-arthritis/)
**Hook B:** *"Those lumps aren't bones. They're chalky crystal deposits."*
```
Those lumps on his fingers aren't bones or tumors.
They're CRYSTALS. 💎😳

This is what untreated GOUT can do.

Gout starts when uric acid builds up in the blood.
At first it causes sudden, brutal attacks — usually in the big toe.
Even the weight of a bedsheet can feel unbearable.

But left untreated for years…

.
.
.

👉 The uric acid forms chalky white deposits called TOPHI — in the joints, under the skin, even in the kidneys.
They slowly destroy the joints and deform the hands and feet.

This patient also had kidney failure, which made it much worse (the kidneys normally flush uric acid out).

🤯 Gout was once called "the disease of kings" — linked to rich food and drink.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: Benjamin Franklin and Henry VIII are both said to have suffered from gout.
🩺 Gout is VERY treatable. Medicines that lower uric acid can stop this — and even shrink tophi.

Do you know anyone who gets gout attacks? 🦶
```

## 2. He ignored this lump for 20 years. It weighed 14 kg ⚖️

```
This lump on his back was there for 20 years.
It weighed 14 kg (31 lbs) — as much as a 3-year-old child. ⚖️😳

The 54-year-old hadn't seen a doctor since he was a child.
The mass on his lower back didn't hurt.
But in the last 3 years, it had nearly DOUBLED in size.

Doctors worried it might be cancer…

.
.
.

👉 It was a LIPOMA — a benign tumor made of fat.
A giant one: 35 × 38 cm.

✅ A 6-hour surgery removed it completely. Surgeons reused his own stretched skin to close the huge wound.

🤯 Lipomas are the most common soft-tissue tumor — most are the size of a grape and never cause problems.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: a lipoma feels soft and rubbery and moves slightly under your finger when you push it.
🩺 Any lump that grows FAST, gets hard, or hurts = get it checked. Most are harmless, but only a doctor can tell.

Why do you think he waited 20 years? 🤔
```

## 3. Hundreds of tumors, and she had them since puberty
📷 `03_neurofibromatosis_back.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/neurofibromatosis-type-i-with-severe-exacerbation-of-asthma/)
**Hook B:** *"Every bump on this back is a tumor growing on a nerve."*
```
Every single bump on this back is a TUMOR. 😳

This woman, 45, has NEUROFIBROMATOSIS TYPE 1 (NF1).

She was born with light-brown "coffee-with-milk" patches on her skin (café-au-lait spots).
Then, from puberty, these soft bumps started appearing — and kept multiplying.

.
.
.

👉 They're neurofibromas: benign tumors that grow on nerves under the skin.
NF1 is a GENETIC condition — a mistake in a gene that normally stops nerve cells from overgrowing.

It can be inherited… or happen as a brand-new mutation in a family with no history of it.

🤯 Despite how it looks, most of these tumors are NOT cancer.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: NF1 affects about 1 in every 3,000 people — more common than many people think.
🩺 Six or more café-au-lait spots in a child = mention it to the pediatrician.

💙 People with NF1 often face stares and comments. Kindness costs nothing.

Had you ever heard of neurofibromatosis? 💬
```

## 4. Soft hair suddenly covered her face. It was CANCER
📷 `04_hair_growth_cancer_sign.jpg` · Original: Kovitwanichkanont, NEJM 2020 · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/excessive-hair-growth-a-sign-of-cancer/)
**Hook B:** *"In 2 months, fine baby-like hair covered her face. Doctors ordered a scan."*
```
In just 2 months, fine soft hair covered her face, ears and neck.
Doctors immediately ordered a cancer scan. 😳

A 51-year-old woman noticed rapidly growing, colorless "baby hair" — the kind newborns have.
She also had a cough, tiredness, and was losing weight.
She had smoked for decades.

.
.
.

👉 The scan found LUNG CANCER that had spread to a lymph node.

This rare skin sign is called "acquired hypertrichosis lanuginosa."
Some tumors release substances that switch hair follicles back on — growing fetal-type hair.
It can appear BEFORE the cancer is found.

✅ After chemotherapy, radiation and immunotherapy, the hair growth dropped dramatically within 2 months — and her cough and weight loss resolved.

🤯 Your skin can be the first organ to warn you about cancer.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: this type of fine hair is called "lanugo" — babies are covered in it before birth.
🩺 Sudden unexplained hair growth + weight loss = see a doctor.

Did you know hair growth could be a cancer warning sign? 💬

#LungCancer #Dermatology #EarlyDetection
```

## 5. Her hands turned ORANGE from her favorite foods 🍊
📷 `05_orange_skin_carotenemia.jpg` · Original: published case report (via MEDizzy) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/yellowish-discolouration-of-the-skin-due-to-spinach-sweet-potatoes-and-oranges/)
**Hook B:** *"A dozen oranges a day. 3 years later, look at her palms."*
```
She ate a DOZEN oranges a day. Look at her palms. 🍊😳

A 32-year-old woman noticed her palms turning yellow-orange.
Then her soles. Then her face — and even her tongue.
Her naturally dark skin was getting lighter and more orange over 3 years.

Liver? Normal. Thyroid? Normal. Blood count? Normal.

.
.
.

👉 Her diet: about 12 oranges a day in season, plus lots of sweet potatoes and spinach.

Diagnosis: CAROTENEMIA — too much beta-carotene (the orange pigment in these foods) building up in the skin.

🤯 Unlike jaundice, carotenemia does NOT turn the whites of the eyes yellow — that's how doctors tell them apart. 👁️
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: babies who eat lots of pureed carrots can turn orange too — it's harmless.
🩺 Yellow skin + yellow EYES = could be the liver → see a doctor. Yellow skin with WHITE eyes = often diet. (Still get checked.)

What's the most you've ever eaten of one food in a day? 🍊
```

## 6. Her fingertips look like drumsticks 🥁
📷 `06_extreme_finger_clubbing.jpg` · via MEDizzy (originally shared by a paramedic) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/extreme-finger-clubbing/)
**Hook B:** *"Doctors can spot lung disease just by looking at your fingertips."*
```
Look at the tips of her fingers. Doctors call this "drumstick fingers." 🥁

A paramedic met this 56-year-old woman while she was waiting for a LUNG TRANSPLANT.

Her fingertips have swollen into rounded clubs, and her nails curve over the tips like upside-down spoons.

.
.
.

👉 This is FINGER CLUBBING — one of the oldest signs in medicine.

Her cause: idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, a disease that slowly scars the lungs.
Clubbing is linked to long-term low oxygen and lung, heart or gut disease.
It can also be a sign of lung CANCER.

🤯 Doctors have been noticing it since Hippocrates — over 2,400 years ago.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: try the "Schamroth window test." Put the nails of your two index fingers back-to-back. If you see a small diamond-shaped gap, that's normal. No gap? Could be clubbing.
🩺 Your fingertips changing shape is NEVER normal — get your lungs and heart checked.

Did you just try the finger test? 👀
```

## 7. Her finger turned completely WHITE in the cold ❄️
📷 `07_white_fingers_raynauds.jpg` · Shared publicly by the patient's daughter on Twitter (via MEDizzy) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/raynauds-syndrome-causes-womans-fingers-to-turn-completely-white/)
**Hook B:** *"Her daughter posted this. Thousands replied: 'I have this too.'"*
```
Her daughter posted this photo online.
Thousands of people replied: "WAIT — I have this too." ❄️😳

Every time the temperature drops, her middle fingers go completely white — like the blood has been switched off.

Even with gloves on, below about 2–3°C it happens anyway.

.
.
.

👉 It's RAYNAUD'S PHENOMENON.

The small arteries in the fingers overreact to cold or stress and clamp shut.
Fingers turn white → then blue → then red as blood rushes back.
That last stage brings what she calls "horrendous pins and needles" — and can take an hour.

🤯 The post got over 32,000 likes from people who didn't know their condition had a name.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: Raynaud's affects an estimated 1 in 20 people — far more common than most realize.
🩺 Keep the whole body warm (not just hands). If it's severe or one-sided, see a doctor — it can be linked to autoimmune diseases.

Do YOUR fingers ever do this? 🧤

#Raynauds #WinterHealth #DidYouKnow
```

## 9. Only the TOP of his body turned blue 💙
📷 `09_quarter_blue_svc.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/quarter-blue-but-why-quarter/)
**Hook B:** *"Look closely. The blue stops at a line across his chest."*
```
His head, neck, chest and arms turned BLUE-purple.
The rest of his body? Normal color. 😳

A 59-year-old man was found unconscious at home and rushed to the ER.
He'd been getting regular medicine for Crohn's disease through a long-term IV line in a vein in his chest.

Look at the photo: the color stops in a line across his upper body.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: SUPERIOR VENA CAVA SYNDROME.

The superior vena cava is the big vein that carries ALL the blood from your head, neck and arms back to the heart.
A blood clot had formed around his IV line and blocked it completely.
Blood from the upper body had nowhere to go — so it pooled, turning everything above the blockage blue.

🤯 That sharp "line" on the body shows exactly where the blocked vein drains.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: the most common cause of SVC syndrome is actually lung cancer pressing on the vein.
🩺 Swelling and redness/blue color of the face, neck or arms + shortness of breath = EMERGENCY.

Had you ever heard of the superior vena cava? 💬
```

## 10. He's only 27. Why is his skin so loose?
📷 `10_saggy_skin_pxe.jpg` · Original: ClinMed International Library · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/bleeding-disorder-and-saggy-skin/)
**Hook B:** *"His dentist found the first clue. His skin told the rest."*
```
He's only 27. Look at the skin on his chest and stomach. 😳

It hangs in loose, deep folds — like someone decades older.

The first clue didn't even come from his skin.
His DENTIST ordered blood tests, and they showed his blood wasn't clotting properly.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: PSEUDOXANTHOMA ELASTICUM (PXE) — a rare genetic disease.

Calcium slowly builds up in the elastic fibers of the body — in the skin, eyes and blood vessels.
The skin loses its "snap back" and starts to sag.
In his case, the same gene problem also affected his vitamin K–dependent clotting factors.

🤯 PXE often runs in families — his relatives had similar signs.
🧠 Fact to impress your friends: an early clue of PXE is small yellowish bumps on the sides of the neck that look like "plucked chicken skin."
🩺 PXE can affect the eyes and heart — people with it need regular eye checks.

Had you ever heard of a disease that makes skin age early? 💬

#RareDisease #Genetics #Dermatology
📷 ClinMed International Library / via MEDizzy
```
