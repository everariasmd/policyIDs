## 1. She carried a "stone baby" for 60 years 🪨👶
📷 `01_stone_baby_ct.jpg` · via MEDizzy · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/calcified-fetus-in-a-91-year-old-womans-belly/)
⚠️ *Pregnancy loss: keep the tone gentle.*
**Hook B:** *"Doctors thought it was a tumor. It was a baby — from 60 years ago."*

```
That's not a tumor. It's a baby — turned to STONE. 🪨

This scan shows what doctors call a LITHOPEDION, a "stone baby."

In one case from Chile, a 91-year-old woman had an X-ray after a fall.
Doctors first thought the lump in her belly was a tumor…
then realized it was a calcified fetus — about 1.9 kg — that had been inside her for more than 60 YEARS. 😳

.
.
.

👉 How is this possible?
Sometimes a pregnancy grows OUTSIDE the womb, in the abdomen.
If the baby dies and is too large for the body to absorb, the body wraps it in calcium to protect itself from infection.
It slowly turns to stone.

Fewer than a few hundred cases have ever been described in medical history.

Because surgery was riskier at her age, doctors left it in place.
She said it reminded her of the child she and her late husband always wanted. 💔
```

## 2. Live worms crawling across his eye 👁️🪱
📷 `02_worms_in_eye.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/ocular-thelaziasis-parasitic-worm-feeding-on-eye-secretions/)
**Hook B:** *"He thought it was dust in his eye. It was MOVING."*

```
He thought there was dust in his eye. Then doctors saw it MOVE. 👁️🪱

A 34-year-old man who lived on a farm had an itchy eye that felt like something was stuck in it.

Under a magnifying eye microscope, doctors lifted his upper eyelid and found…

.
.
.

👉 TWO LIVE WORMS — living on the surface of his eye. 😳

They're called Thelazia callipaeda, the "Oriental eye worm."
They feed on TEARS and eye secretions.

How they get there: tiny fruit flies that hover around the eyes of dogs (and sometimes people) carry the larvae — and drop them off while drinking tears. 🪰
He lived on a farm with his dog.

✅ Doctors numbed the eye and pulled the worms out with fine forceps. A month later, the itching was gone.

🩺 Persistent "something in my eye" feeling? Get it checked — especially if you live around animals.

Would you have stayed calm? 😬
```

```
She had recently been bitten by lots of mosquitoes in the countryside outside Moscow.

✅ The worm was removed (photo D).
```

## 4. A worm was living in her tonsil after eating sashimi 🍣
📷 `04_worm_in_tonsil.jpg` · Original: Fukui et al., *Am J Trop Med Hyg* 2020 · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/worm-found-living-inside-womans-tonsils/)
**Hook B:** *"5 days of sore throat. Doctors saw something wriggling."*

```
Her sore throat started right after eating sashimi. Then doctors looked in her mouth… 🍣😳

A 25-year-old woman in Tokyo had 5 days of throat pain and irritation.
In her LEFT TONSIL, doctors saw something black… and wriggling.

.
.
.

👉 A live parasitic worm — 3.8 cm long. (That's it on the ruler.)

It was a larva of Pseudoterranova azarasi, a roundworm that lives in fish and marine mammals like seals.
It had come from the raw fish she ate.

✅ Doctors pulled it out with tweezers. Her symptoms disappeared right away.

🐟 Freezing fish properly before eating it raw kills these parasites — that's why sushi-grade fish is frozen first.

Sushi lovers… still hungry? 😅
```

## 5. Doctors found a FETUS inside a baby's brain 🧠
📷 `05_fetus_in_brain_ct.jpg` · Original: published case report (intracranial fetus in fetu) · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/intracranial-fetus-in-fetu/)
⚠️ *Infant patient. The image is a CT scan only.*
**Hook B:** *"See the bones (arrow)? They belong to a twin — inside her head."*

```
Look at the arrow. Those are BONES — inside a 1-year-old's brain. 🧠😳

A baby girl's head kept growing too big.
At 1 year old, she couldn't sit up or stand.

The CT scan showed something no one expected inside her skull:
soft tissue… a spine… and LIMB-like bones.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: FETUS IN FETU — a "parasitic twin."

Very early in pregnancy, a twin can become trapped INSIDE the body of the other twin.
It never develops properly, but it can grow bones, a spine and limbs.
Most are found in the belly. Inside the brain is extremely rare.

It was pressing on her brain and blocking the normal fluid flow — making her head swell.

✅ Neurosurgeons removed it completely.

🩺 A baby's head growing unusually fast always needs to be checked.

Had you ever heard of a twin growing INSIDE its sibling? 🤯
```

```
Doctors were scanning this man's belly for something else… and saw this MOVING. 🪱

A 20-year-old in New Delhi came in with stomach pain, diarrhea and vomiting.
Doctors did an ultrasound to check how dehydrated he was.

Instead, the screen showed long, tube-shaped things curling and moving INSIDE his stomach. 😳

.
.
.

👉 ROUNDWORMS (Ascaris lumbricoides).

His stool sample confirmed it — full of roundworm eggs.
Ascaris is one of the most common parasites on Earth, infecting hundreds of millions of people worldwide.

✅ A single dose of a deworming tablet. He went home the next day.

🧼 Prevention: wash hands, wash vegetables, drink safe water.

Have you ever taken a deworming tablet? 💊
```

## 8. Her heart was trapped in a shell of STONE 🫀🪨
📷 `08_armored_heart.jpg` · Original: NEJM · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/constrictive-pericarditis-in-a-69-year-old/)
**Hook B:** *"See the bright white curve around her heart? That's calcium — a shell."*

```
See the bright white line wrapped around this heart? It's a SHELL. 🫀🪨

A 69-year-old woman had been short of breath for 3 YEARS.
Her legs and body were swelling with fluid.

Her X-ray and CT showed the sac around her heart (the pericardium) had turned thick and CALCIFIED — hard as bone.

.
.
.

👉 Diagnosis: Constrictive pericarditis — doctors call it an "armored heart."

The stiff shell squeezed her heart so it couldn't fill properly with blood.
The likely cause? ASBESTOS — she had worked at an insulation factory years before.

✅ Surgeons peeled the shell off her heart (pericardiectomy). 2 years later, she was much better.

🩺 Asbestos damage can show up DECADES after exposure. If you worked in construction, shipyards or insulation, tell your doctor.

Did anyone in your family work with asbestos? 💬
```

## 9. Tapeworm cysts were eating her SPINE 🦴
📷 `09_tapeworm_spine_mri.jpg` · Original: Jacquier & Piroth, NEJM 2018 · [source](https://journal.medizzy.com/doctors-found-parasites-in-womans-spine/)
**Hook B:** *"Electric shocks down her legs. The MRI showed bubbles in her spine."*

```
Electric shocks running down her legs. She kept falling. The MRI showed THIS. 🦴

A 35-year-old woman in France had growing weakness and couldn't ride her horse anymore.
Her MRI showed a bubbly lesion eating into one of her vertebrae (look at the middle of the spine).

.
.
.

👉 TAPEWORM cysts. 😳

It was hydatid disease (Echinococcus granulosus) — a tapeworm carried by dogs and farm animals.
People get infected by swallowing tiny eggs from contaminated food, water or soil.
The larvae form fluid-filled cysts — usually in the liver or lungs. In the spine, it's rare (only a few % of cases).

She lived in France, hadn't traveled, and had contact with horses and cattle — the exact source was never found.

✅ Surgery + anti-parasitic medication. 9 months later, no symptoms.

🐕 Deworm dogs regularly, and wash hands and produce well.

Had you ever heard of tapeworms in the spine? 💬
```

```
Tests pointed to an inflamed gallbladder — usually caused by stones.

.
.
.

👉 No stones.

✅ After surgery and a tapeworm-killing medicine, her pain was gone and she went home a week later.

🥩 Cook beef and pork thoroughly.
```
